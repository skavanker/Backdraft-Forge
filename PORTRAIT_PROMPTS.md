Base format: `[Race] [gender] [class], fantasy portrait, [class features], [race features], aged 30-40, parchment background, warm lighting, painterly style --ar 1:1 --style raw --s 150`

**Filename format:** `[Race][Gender][Class].png` (e.g., `HumanMaleFighter.png`)
